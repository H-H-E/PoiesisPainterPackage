What this comparison does—and doesn't—prove

Measured times are from one local browser run and include different orchestration overhead. They are not statistically controlled performance benchmarks or independent judgments of artistic quality. Basic Canvas2D is deliberately limited; winning against it does not prove superiority to p5.brush, Washes, wet-edge, MyPaint or another specialized engine. Those require their actual engines running the same subject under calibrated, transparent conditions.

The framework can accept p5.brush and perfect-freehand through optional adapters when supplied by a consumer. Neither is bundled or executed in this page. See the integration guide.
